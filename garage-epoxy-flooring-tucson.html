Heat Is the Design Constraint

Concrete in direct Tucson sun runs far above air temperature, and a closed garage bakes all afternoon and gives the heat back overnight. That matters twice: once during installation, and once for the life of the floor.

During installation, resin cures faster as temperature climbs. Working time that would be twenty minutes in a mild climate collapses to a handful, and a coating can skin over before it levels, leaving roller marks that will not flow out. The answer is scheduling rather than product marketing: we start at first light, move air through the space, keep material out of the sun until the moment it is mixed, and select a slower hardener in summer.

For the life of the floor, heat is why cheap systems fail here and survive elsewhere. A film that never reached full hardness stays slightly soft, and soft plus hot tires is the whole failure mechanism.

The Local Failure Mode

Hot Tire Pickup, Explained Properly

This is the single most common way a Tucson garage floor dies, and it is almost always misdiagnosed as a bad product batch.

A tire that has been running on 150-degree asphalt holds that heat for a long time after you park. Sitting on a coating, it does two things: it softens the film directly under the contact patch, and as the rubber cools it contracts and grips. Back the car out the next morning and the coating comes with it, in patches the exact shape of your tread.

It is not caused by heat alone. It is caused by heat acting on a film that never reached full hardness, either because the product was thin, because it cured too fast in summer, or because it never bonded properly to begin with. A correctly built system with a polyaspartic or polyurea wear layer simply does not do this.

Which is why we will not quote a roll-on kit here. Those are the floors we get called to grind off.

Sun Through an Open Door

Tucson gets more usable sunshine than nearly anywhere in the United States, and low winter sun reaches deep into a garage with the door up. Standard clear epoxy is not UV stable. It ambers, then chalks, and the change is gradual enough that people do not notice until they move a cabinet and see the original color underneath.

The remedy is a UV-stable polyaspartic clear as the last coat, over whatever body coat the floor needs. It holds color indefinitely, and it happens to be the same layer that solves hot tire pickup, so on most Tucson garages it earns its cost twice.

If your door faces west, treat that clear coat as mandatory rather than an upgrade. If the garage is north-facing and the door is usually closed, a conventional clear is defensible and cheaper, and we will tell you so.

What We Actually Do to the Slab

Everything above is irrelevant if the coating is not mechanically locked into the concrete.

We diamond grind the entire slab, including the perimeter with a hand machine, and vacuum the dust through HEPA collection. No acid etching — it leaves residue and profiles unevenly, and it is the reason most peeled floors peeled.

Cracks get chased open and filled. Tucson slabs move for their own reasons: caliche layers under the pad, expansive soils in parts of the valley, and the sheer thermal range across a year. Static cracks disappear under the coating. A crack still moving gets a flexible filler and may telegraph faintly, and we say which one you have before you pay.

Then base coat, decorative flake broadcast if you chose it, scrape and vacuum, and the clear. Most two-car garages are a single day on site.

Garage Floor Epoxy FAQ — Tucson, AZ

What is hot tire pickup and why is it worse in Tucson?

Tires come off Tucson asphalt in July genuinely hot, and they soften any coating they sit on. A weak or under-cured film sticks to the tread and lifts off in patches shaped like your tires. Full-cure systems with a polyaspartic or polyurea wear layer are immune to it; thin big-box kits fail this way almost every summer.

Can you install a garage floor in Tucson in the summer?

Yes, but the schedule shifts. A closed garage slab can push past 100°F by afternoon, which shortens working time to a few minutes and can flash-cure the surface before it levels. We start at dawn, run fans, and pick a slower hardener. Anyone quoting a midday July pour without mentioning slab temperature has not done many.

Will the sun coming in the door yellow my floor?

Clear epoxy will amber under Tucson UV faster than almost anywhere in the country, and a west-facing door left open in the afternoon accelerates it. The fix is a UV-stable polyaspartic clear as the final coat. It costs more and it is the difference between a floor that looks the same in year five and one that has gone tan.

How much does a garage floor coating cost in Tucson?

Price depends mostly on garage size, the system you pick, and the condition of the slab. Slab grinding is included; removing a failed previous coating is not and is quoted as a separate line. We give every homeowner a free, itemized on-site estimate.
